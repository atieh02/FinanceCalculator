How the comparison works

Both methods use the same basic mechanic: make each debt’s minimum payment, then direct the available extra payment toward one target debt. When that debt is paid off, its freed-up payment is rolled into the next target.

Avalanche method

The avalanche method orders debts by highest interest rate first. Because high-rate balances receive the extra payment earlier, this approach will often reduce total interest compared with paying lower-rate balances first, assuming the same debts, rates, and payment amount.

Snowball method

The snowball method orders debts by smallest balance first. The mathematical priority is not the interest rate; the goal is to create earlier payoff milestones. Those quick wins can make a repayment plan feel more tangible for some people.

Why compare both?

There is no need to pretend the choice is purely mathematical. Avalanche can save interest, while snowball can provide earlier visible wins. Enter your actual debts to see the trade-off for your numbers.
