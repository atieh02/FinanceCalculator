Common questions

Are these calculators free?
Yes. Every calculator on CalcMyFin is free to use with no sign-up, no email and no limits.
Is my information private?
Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations run in your browser, and we don't collect or store the values you enter.
How accurate are the results?
Each calculator uses standard, published formulas, and we show the math on every page. Results are educational estimates: lenders, insurers and tax rules can change the real numbers.
Is this financial advice?
No. CalcMyFin provides educational tools to help you explore scenarios. For decisions about insurance, taxes, investing or major debts, consider talking with a qualified professional.
