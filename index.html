Why these tools are simple

Money decisions can feel complicated without needing to be. These calculators focus on transparent formulas, sensible inputs, and results you can inspect. They do not require an account, collect a form submission, or recommend financial products.

Use a calculator as a first pass, then consider the parts of your situation that a formula cannot capture. For important insurance, tax, investment, or debt decisions, qualified professional advice may be appropriate.
