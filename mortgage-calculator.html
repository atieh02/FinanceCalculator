What this mortgage calculator includes

The principal-and-interest payment uses the standard fixed-rate loan formula. The headline monthly estimate can also include annual property tax, annual homeowners insurance, monthly HOA fees, and an optional PMI assumption.

Why taxes and insurance matter

A mortgage payment is not always the whole cost of owning a home. The CFPB specifically advises buyers to consider property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance where applicable, HOA fees, maintenance, and other housing costs when judging affordability.

Test the assumptions

Change the down payment, interest rate, or term and watch the payment move. A lower rate or longer term can reduce the required monthly principal-and-interest payment, while a longer term can increase total interest. The result is an estimate, not a lending approval or affordability determination.

Educational estimate only. It does not account for every lender fee, closing cost, adjustable-rate feature, tax rule, insurance quote, or local housing expense.
