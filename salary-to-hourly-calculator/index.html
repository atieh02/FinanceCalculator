How to use the salary to hourly calculator

  1. Enter your pay and choose whether it's per hour, week, month or year.
  2. Set how many hours you work per week and weeks per year.
  3. Read your pay at every other interval in the results.

How it's calculated

Everything is converted through an annual figure. A full-time schedule of 40 hours × 52 weeks is 2,080 hours a year, so:

Hourly = Annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Daily pay assumes a 5-day week, biweekly is annual ÷ 26 and monthly is annual ÷ 12.

Example

A $75,000 salary at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is about $36.06 an hour, $1,442 a week, $2,885 every two weeks and $6,250 a month, all before taxes.

Tips

  • A quick rule: $1 per hour ≈ $2,000 per year for full-time work.
  • Compare total compensation, including benefits, 401(k) match and paid time off, not just salary.
  • Salaried roles with long hours can have a lower effective hourly rate than they appear.
  • These are gross (pre-tax) figures. Take-home pay will be lower after taxes and deductions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert salary to hourly?
Divide your annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard 40-hour week that's 2,080 hours, so a $60,000 salary is about $28.85 per hour.
How many work hours are in a year?
40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. If you get two weeks of unpaid time off, it's 40 × 50 = 2,000 hours. Adjust the weeks per year above to match.
Is this before or after taxes?
Before taxes (gross pay). Federal, state and payroll taxes, plus deductions like health insurance and retirement contributions, reduce your take-home pay.
How much is $20 an hour per year?
At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $20 an hour is $41,600 a year before taxes, about $3,467 a month.
