Why the multiplier is editable

A simple income multiple is useful for orientation, but households have different obligations and resources. Making the multiplier adjustable lets you test how sensitive the estimate is to that assumption instead of presenting 10× as if it were a precise rule.

What the other inputs mean

Debts represent balances that may remain. Future costs represent obligations you expect survivors to face. Existing savings, investments, and current life insurance reduce the amount suggested by the formula. Use current, supportable estimates rather than numbers chosen to produce a desired answer.

Use the result carefully

This is a starting estimate, not a policy recommendation. A full needs analysis may account for dependents, employer coverage, income replacement duration, taxes, inflation, and policy-specific features.

This calculator is a general educational estimate, not personalized financial, insurance, tax, or legal advice.
