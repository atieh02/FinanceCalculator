Why months are adjustable

There is no universal emergency-fund size. Stable income, household structure, job volatility, and access to other resources can all affect how much cash feels appropriate. This tool makes the assumption explicit.

What counts as essential?

Use costs you would need to keep paying during an income disruption: housing, utilities, basic food, insurance, minimum debt payments, required transportation, and other necessities. Optional spending generally belongs outside the emergency-fund budget.

Try multiple scenarios

Run 3, 6, 9, and 12-month scenarios. You can also change the essential-expense number to model a leaner or more expensive baseline. The result is a planning target, not a requirement.
