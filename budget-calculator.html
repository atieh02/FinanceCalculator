What the categories mean

Needs are necessary costs such as housing, utilities, basic food, insurance, and required debt payments. Wants are discretionary spending. Savings can include emergency savings, retirement contributions, and additional debt reduction depending on how you choose to structure your plan.

Why the percentages are editable

50/30/20 is a starting framework, not a law. High housing costs, debt, family needs, or aggressive savings goals may justify another split. The calculator keeps the percentages visible and requires them to total 100%.

Compare with actual spending

After choosing a scenario, compare each category with your real spending. If the plan does not fit, change the assumptions rather than treating the template as a pass-or-fail test.
