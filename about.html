About

Simple tools for everyday money questions

Clear Money Tools exists to make common financial calculations easier to understand.

What we build

The site provides small, transparent calculators for questions people regularly face: mortgage and loan payments, compound growth, retirement scenarios, credit-card payoff, debt strategies, budgeting, emergency funds, life insurance estimates, and net worth snapshots.

There are no accounts, paid tiers, or saved histories. Calculations happen in the browser so the numbers you enter are not sent to a site server.

What we do not do

These tools do not recommend specific insurance products, investments, tax strategies, or lenders. They are educational calculators, not a substitute for advice tailored to your circumstances.

Why the site is intentionally restrained

Financial decisions can already carry enough uncertainty. The goal is to make the formulas visible, the language plain, and the interface calm. Where a calculation is only a rule of thumb, the site says so.
