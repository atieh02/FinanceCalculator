What compound interest means

Compounding means previously earned growth can itself participate in future growth. This calculator combines a starting balance with regular contributions and applies the selected constant rate at the selected frequency.

Why the result is only a projection

An assumed return is not a promise. Investment returns can rise and fall, and fees, taxes, inflation, contribution timing, and changes in the rate can materially affect the actual outcome. Use several rates to create a range rather than treating one result as certain.

Make the assumptions visible

Try changing the contribution from $500 to $750, or the rate from 7% to 5% or 9%. The point is not to predict the future perfectly; it is to understand how sensitive the result is to the assumptions you control.

Educational projection only. It is not investment advice or a guarantee of future returns.
