How the payment is calculated

For a fixed-rate loan, the standard amortization formula spreads the principal and interest across the selected number of monthly payments. A 0% rate is handled separately: principal is divided evenly across the months.

What the extra-payment control does

The base payment remains the scheduled payment for the original term. The extra-payment field then simulates adding more each month and reports the resulting payoff time and interest. This makes it easy to test whether a slightly larger payment materially changes the timeline.

Before using a quote

Actual loans can include origination fees, penalties, variable rates, insurance, or other costs. Compare the calculator's assumptions with the lender's actual disclosure before making a decision.
