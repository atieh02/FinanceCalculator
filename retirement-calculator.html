What this retirement calculator models

The projection compounds the current balance, adds monthly contributions, and adds an employer contribution based on the salary percentage you enter. Contributions and salary are modeled to rise with the salary-growth assumption.

Why return and inflation are separate

A nominal return and inflation are not the same thing. The calculator shows an approximate real return using the standard relationship between the two assumptions. This is useful for scenario testing, but it does not model taxes, fees, changing asset allocations, Social Security, pensions, or sequence-of-returns risk.

Run multiple scenarios

Try a lower return, a higher contribution, or a later retirement age and compare the change. A range of scenarios is more informative than a single precise-looking number.
