Why minimum payments can be misleading

The minimum payment is designed to keep the account current under the issuer's rules; it is not necessarily a fast payoff plan. This calculator shows how adding a fixed extra amount changes the modeled payoff timeline and interest.

Target a payoff period

The target-payment result is separate from the minimum-plus-extra simulation. It answers a different question: “What fixed monthly payment would amortize this balance over the number of months I entered?” Real card issuers can use different minimum-payment formulas, fees, and transaction rules.

Use your actual APR

Credit card rates can be high enough that small changes in payment size materially affect the timeline. Try several extra-payment amounts rather than assuming the first result is optimal.

Educational estimate only. Actual card statements can include fees, changing rates, new purchases, and issuer-specific minimum-payment rules.
