Privacy

Privacy policy

A plain-language overview of what happens to information entered into this site.

Calculator inputs

All calculator inputs are processed entirely client-side in your browser. Clear Money Tools does not transmit these inputs to or store them on a site server. There is no calculator account, database, or saved-history feature.

Advertising

Advertising may be added in the future through Google AdSense, after approval. When active, Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to personalize or measure advertising, subject to Google's applicable policies and your available settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

This static site is designed without a backend. If external services are introduced later, this policy should be updated to describe what they collect and why.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the site through the Contact page.
